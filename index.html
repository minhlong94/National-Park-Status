Seasonal road history

This section shows the opening and closing dates of the large park roads that snow closes each winter. It shows only the last 10 years, with the newest year first. Each bar is one season. The bottom of the bar is the day the road opened. The top of the bar is the day the road closed for winter.

Closure history

This table shows the major full and partial closures from the last 5 years, with the newest first. It also shows shutdowns of all parks. The data comes from NPS news releases and news reports. The list is not complete. Refresh does not change it.

Click a park row to see all its alerts, its closure history and a 3-day forecast. The seasonal road dates come from the NPS Yosemite and Glacier datasets and from Rocky Mountain press releases. The park status comes from official NPS alerts (developer.nps.gov). The page sorts each alert into a type by keywords. Read the alert text before you drive. The weather comes from Open-Meteo. The warnings come from the National Weather Service (api.weather.gov). The visits are recreation visits in calendar year 2025, from NPS Visitor Use Statistics. NPS gives Sequoia and Kings Canyon as separate numbers. Temperatures are in °F. Wind speeds are in mph.
